## Groovemap

A living encyclopedia of 4.1k+ grooves across 35+ style families — every beat playable in your browser, dissected track by track, then exported as MIDI, audio or a share link for your own music.

Groovemap is three tools sharing one engine. An encyclopedia of drum patterns — every groove documented with its construction, each instrument's role, variations, provenance and related patterns. A laboratory (Groove Lab) where any pattern can be loaded, edited step by step, transformed, and taken away as MIDI, audio, JSON or a share link. And a learning platform — the Rhythm School lessons and curated collections that teach the vocabulary the catalog is written in.
